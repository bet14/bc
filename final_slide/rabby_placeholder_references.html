References & Sources

Academic Papers

Cite 1
Hu, X., He, N., & Wang, H. (2025). WalletProbe: A Testing Framework for Browser-based Cryptocurrency Wallet Extensions. arXiv:2504.11735.
April 2025

Presents a mutation-based testing framework that identified 13 attack vectors and 21 attack strategies across 39 browser-based wallets. Both MetaMask and Rabby accepted the researchers' responsible disclosure and adopted fixes. Evaluates transaction simulators, security alert modules, and wallet UI components.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.11735
Cite 2
Guan, S., & Li, K. (2025). Ethereum Crypto Wallets under Address Poisoning: How Usable and Secure Are They? arXiv:2508.12107.
August 2025

Systematically evaluates 53 popular Ethereum wallets against address poisoning attacks. Finds that millions of users were targeted with losses exceeding $100M. Specifically notes Rabby's effectiveness in flagging poisoned transfers as scam transactions, distinguishing it from wallets that display fake transfers without warning.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12107
Cite 3
Qin, Y., & Duan, H. (2026). "What I Sign Is Not What I See": Towards Explainable and Trustworthy Cryptocurrency Wallet Signatures. arXiv:2601.16751.
January 2026

Investigates the usability-security gap in wallet signatures through formative studies. Reveals that users frequently misread critical parameters and underestimate high-risk signatures. References MetaMask and Rabby as wallets addressing this problem. Proposes a Signature Semantic Decoder framework for plain-language explanations of signing requests.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.16751
Cite 4
Wilczynski, A., & Jasnosz, G. (2025). Security Assessment of Smart Contract Integration and Wallet Interaction in Decentralized Applications. Applied Sciences, 15(15), 8473.
2025

Provides a security assessment framework for dApp-wallet interactions. References Rabby and OKX Wallet as examples of wallets with advanced security interfaces. Discusses transaction simulation accuracy and security guarantees through fraud proofs in decentralized application contexts.

https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/15/15/8473
Cite 5
Soleti, M., Gangwal, A., & Conti, M. (2025). Attacking Anonymity Set in Tornado Cash via Wallet Fingerprints. In Proceedings of the 40th ACM/SIGAPP Symposium on Applied Computing.
2025

Analyzes wallet fingerprinting techniques across multiple wallet extensions including Rabby Wallet. Demonstrates how wallet-specific behaviors can be identified, which has implications for both privacy and security research in the cryptocurrency ecosystem.

https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3672608.3707896
