1. Academic papers

Five recent papers that directly study, benchmark, or reference Rabby Wallet's security behavior.

Cite 1 · April 2025
Hu, X., He, N., & Wang, H. (2025). WalletProbe: A Testing Framework for Browser-based Cryptocurrency Wallet Extensions. arXiv:2504.11735.

A mutation-based testing framework that identified 13 attack vectors and 21 attack strategies across 39 browser-based wallets. Both MetaMask and Rabby accepted the researchers' responsible disclosure and adopted fixes. Evaluates transaction simulators, security alert modules, and wallet UI components.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.11735
Cite 2 · August 2025
Guan, S., & Li, K. (2025). Ethereum Crypto Wallets under Address Poisoning: How Usable and Secure Are They? arXiv:2508.12107.

Systematically evaluates 53 popular Ethereum wallets against address-poisoning attacks, finding that millions of users were targeted with losses exceeding $100M. Specifically notes Rabby's effectiveness in flagging poisoned transfers as scam transactions — distinguishing it from wallets that display fake transfers without warning.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.12107
Cite 3 · January 2026
Qin, Y., & Duan, H. (2026). "What I Sign Is Not What I See": Towards Explainable and Trustworthy Cryptocurrency Wallet Signatures. arXiv:2601.16751.

Investigates the usability-security gap in wallet signatures through formative studies, revealing that users frequently misread critical parameters and underestimate high-risk signatures. References MetaMask and Rabby as wallets addressing this problem, and proposes a Signature Semantic Decoder framework for plain-language explanations of signing requests.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.16751
Cite 4 · 2025
Wilczynski, A., & Jasnosz, G. (2025). Security Assessment of Smart Contract Integration and Wallet Interaction in Decentralized Applications. Applied Sciences, 15(15), 8473.

A security-assessment framework for dApp–wallet interactions that references Rabby and OKX Wallet as examples of wallets with advanced security interfaces, and discusses transaction-simulation accuracy and security guarantees through fraud proofs in decentralized application contexts.

https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/15/15/8473
Cite 5 · 2025
Soleti, M., Gangwal, A., & Conti, M. (2025). Attacking Anonymity Set in Tornado Cash via Wallet Fingerprints. In Proceedings of the 40th ACM/SIGAPP Symposium on Applied Computing.

Analyzes wallet-fingerprinting techniques across multiple wallet extensions, including Rabby Wallet, demonstrating how wallet-specific behaviors can be identified — with implications for both privacy and security research in the cryptocurrency ecosystem.

https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3672608.3707896
